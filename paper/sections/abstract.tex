\begin{abstract}
Text-to-image diffusion models often produce plausible images that violate part of the prompt: an object is missing, a colour lands on the wrong object, or a count, a relative size or a spatial relation is wrong.
We ask \emph{when} along the denoising trajectory each kind of constraint stops depending on the remaining randomness, \emph{what} classifier-free guidance (CFG) changes about that timing, whether cross-attention reveals it without labels, and whether a guidance schedule timed by the measurement is useful.
On Stable Diffusion~1.5 and a controlled scene-graph benchmark we measure a chance-corrected restart-persistence curve $C(t)$ and its half-point $t_{50}$ per constraint type by re-noising saved trajectory states. We also introduce Commitment-Aligned Guidance (CAG), which switches from high to low guidance after the development-set $t_{50}$.
Four hypotheses, decision rules and all development-set constants were frozen in a signed protocol before any test image was generated. A blinded human audit validated the automatic judge only for object presence (accuracy \accPres); two development gates failed; we report all of this as found.
Six of nine test shards exist, so every test result here is \emph{partial and exploratory}.
On them, stronger guidance makes object presence persistent earlier: $t_{50}$ falls from \rbTwoPres{} steps at $w=2$ to \rbFifteenPres{} at $w=15$ (registered contrast $\rbHdReg$ steps, 95\% CI $[\rbHdRegLo,\rbHdRegHi]$, Holm $p=\rbHdHolm$). The effect is not produced by the chance correction, but an independent-error stress test at the audited judge error removes it.
Constraint-type orderings measured on the development split do not replicate on the test scenes, attention stabilisation does not track persistence (ordering concordance \rbHfConc), and CAG is neither non-inferior to constant CFG nor better than its reversed schedule.
Dose-matched controls explain CAG's diversity gain by its lower mean guidance rather than its timing.
The protocol, estimator, judge audit and partial-test pipeline are documented so that the registered analysis can be completed unchanged once the missing shards exist.
\end{abstract}
