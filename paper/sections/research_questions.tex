\section{Hypotheses and pre-registration}\label{sec:rq}
The four hypotheses form one Holm family ($\alpha=0.05$). A hypothesis is reported \emph{supported} only if its own decision rule passes \emph{and} its Holm-adjusted $p<0.05$, with Holm computed over all four hypotheses even when one is not estimable. Definitions, decision rules and development-set constants were fixed in a protocol addendum and a signed protocol file before any test image was generated (\frozenFiles{} code, configuration, benchmark and documentation files hashed; Section~\ref{sec:repro}).

\paragraph{HA, constraint-specific persistence.} Primary estimand $\Delta t_{50}=t_{50}^{\mathrm{bind\mid pres}}-t_{50}^{\mathrm{pres}}$ at $w=7.5$ with the fixed-jump restart (D8). Support: the 95\% scene-bootstrap interval excludes $0$, $|\Delta t_{50}|\ge3$ steps, and the joint-binding companion and the equal-ratio restart (ER30) agree in sign. Bounded null: the interval lies inside $(-1.5,1.5)$.
\paragraph{HD, CFG moves persistence.} $\Delta t_{50}^{\mathrm{CFG}}=t_{50}(w{=}15)-t_{50}(w{=}2)$ for presence and for binding given presence, with chance agreement computed separately at each $w$ and one shared scene resample. Supported if at least one estimand is negative with $|\Delta|\ge3$ and an interval excluding $0$; $p_{\mathrm{HD}}=\min(1,2\min p)$. A positive interval is reported as contradicting HD.
\paragraph{HF, attention stabilisation.} Supported if the ordering of constraint types by $t_{\mathrm{stab}}$ is concordant with their ordering by $t_{50}$ (concordance $\ge0.8$) and the within-type Spearman correlation between scene $t_{\mathrm{stab}}$ and the scene's area under $C(t)$ has a 95\% interval above $0$ for at least two types. Rejected if concordance is at most $0.5$, or if every within-type interval contains $0$ with upper bound below $0.15$; otherwise inconclusive.
\paragraph{HE, Commitment-Aligned Guidance.} Supported only if all three hold (intersection--union, $p$ = maximum of the three one-sided $p$): adherence $F$ of CAG (A2) is non-inferior to constant CFG~7.5 (A1) (90\% interval lower bound $>-0.03$), seed diversity is higher (95\% interval $>0$), and A2 beats the reversed schedule A4 with equal mean guidance on $F$ (95\% interval $>0$).

\paragraph{Status fixed before the test split.} Development-set evidence (Section~\ref{sec:resDEV}) fixed the following before any test image existed (Table~\ref{tab:status}): HA is exploratory because its precision gate failed; per-type results for count, binding, spatial relation and relative size are exploratory because their judge accuracy missed the audit criterion; HD can be supported only through its presence estimand; HE and HF keep their registered rules, and their reports disclose that $F$ and the type orderings include judge types below the criterion.
