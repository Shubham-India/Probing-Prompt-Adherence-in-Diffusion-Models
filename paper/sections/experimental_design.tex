\section{Experimental design and execution}\label{sec:design}
All jobs come from deterministic manifests sharded by scene group over three workers; a guard refuses any stage whose owner-controlled gates are false, and TEST jobs are refused unless a signed protocol verifies against the code, configurations, benchmark, judge and DEV-derived constants. DEV runs first (restart branches at CFG~7.5 with fixed-jump, equal-ratio and full-resample variants; CFG~2/15 bases; prompt switches; CAG quality guard), then the human evaluator audit and the freeze, then TEST (core and CFG$\times$steps generation; restart branches at three CFG levels; switches; CAG arms; the size family). The manifests total about 27{,}200--28{,}700 full-generation equivalents (the CAG part depends on the DEV-derived switch steps), about 47--62 hours on two T4 GPUs at the measured 6.2--7.8~s per image.
\paragraph{Statistics.} Scene-level bootstrap (10{,}000 draws, fixed keys), common random numbers across conditions, conditional and joint outcomes side by side, exclusions logged.
