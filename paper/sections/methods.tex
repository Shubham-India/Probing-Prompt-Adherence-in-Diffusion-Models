\section{Methods}\label{sec:methods}
\paragraph{Generator.} Stable Diffusion~1.5 (fp16, pinned revision; its fp16 weights are byte-identical to the current hub revision), DDIM $\eta=0$, 40 steps, $512^2$, empty negative prompt, CFG~7.5 for the core condition. The model is used for inference only.
\paragraph{Saved states and re-noising.} The fp16 latent after $t\in\{6,12,18,24,30,36\}$ completed steps is stored. A branch moves to step index $j=\max(t-8,0)$ with fresh branch-specific noise,
\begin{equation}
x'_{j}=\sqrt{\bar\alpha(j)/\bar\alpha(t)}\,x_t+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha(j)/\bar\alpha(t)}\,\varepsilon,
\end{equation}
where $\bar\alpha(s)$ is taken at the scheduler timestep of step $s$, and is denoised deterministically at the base's CFG. The noise ratio varies with $t$ (0.21 at $t=6$, 0.68 at $t=36$), so absolute $t_{50}$ is relative to the probe; an equal-ratio variant ($\approx0.30$ at every $t$) is a required sensitivity analysis.
\paragraph{Prompt switching.} A counterfactual trajectory (colour swap, relation reversal or size swap) is continued from its saved latent with the canonical prompt; $C_{\mathrm{switch}}(t)=\sum_s[W_s(t)-W0_s]/\sum_s[W1_s-W0_s]$, with $W$ the share of images satisfying the counterfactual constraint, $W0$ that share among canonical images and $W1$ among counterfactual images.
\paragraph{Persistence estimator.} $C_\kappa(t)=\sum_s[A_{\kappa s}(t)-A0_{\kappa s}]/\sum_s[1-A0_{\kappa s}]$, with $A$ the agreement of branches with their base and $A0$ the agreement of independent seeds; $t_{50}$ is the interpolated first crossing of $0.5$.
\paragraph{Attention stabilisation.} Cross-attention maps of the constraint words are recorded at every step (conditional branch, heads averaged, layers averaged within the $16\times16$ and $32\times32$ groups). Signals: noun map (presence), colour-word map (binding), ratio of effective areas (size), angle between noun centroids (spatial), connected components (count). Velocity $v(t)=1-\cos(a_t,a_{t-1})$ or the absolute change of a scalar; $t_{\mathrm{stab}}$ is the half path-length step. Recording was checked to leave the generated image unchanged on a real GPU.
\paragraph{CAG.} A per-step guidance schedule in the same DDIM loop; steps with $w=1$ evaluate only the conditional branch, and the number of UNet evaluations is counted per image. With a constant schedule the loop reproduces the pipeline image exactly (checked on a real GPU). Arms: constant~7.5, CAG, a global switch step, the reversed schedule with equal mean guidance, and constant guidance at CAG's mean.
\paragraph{Outcomes.} Presence, count, binding given presence, spatial given presence, size given presence (box area of the larger object at least $1.5\times$ the other's), and joint outcomes; overall satisfaction $F$ is the mean of the families present.
