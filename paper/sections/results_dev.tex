\section{Development-split results}\label{sec:resDEV}
Everything in this section uses DEV scenes only and was fixed before any test image was generated. It is exploratory; its role is to set constants and to decide, through pre-declared gates, which claims the test split can carry.

\paragraph{An estimator error found and corrected before the test split.} The chance-agreement term $A0$ had first been computed from every core image of a scene, including paraphrases, counterfactual variants and other guidance scales, instead of only independent seeds of the branches' own base prompt as specified. This lowers $A0$ and biases every $C(t)$ upwards. The code was corrected, a regression test added, and the DEV constants were recomputed from the stored judgements; both versions are archived. All DEV numbers below use the corrected estimator.

\paragraph{Persistence curves.} Figure~\ref{fig:devcurves} shows $C(t)$ on \devScenesHA{} DEV scenes. Presence crosses $0.5$ near $t_{50}=\tPres$ steps (D8); colour binding given presence is already above $0.5$ at the first grid step (left-censored, $t_{50}\le\tBind$), and count is latest ($\tCount$). With both restart probes the binding-minus-presence difference is negative but its interval includes zero: D8 $\Delta t_{50}=\dtDeight$ $[\dtDeightLo,\dtDeightHi]$, ER30 $\dtErThirty$ $[\dtErThirtyLo,\dtErThirtyHi]$ (Table~\ref{tab:dev-t50}). Left-censoring means DEV cannot place binding relative to presence; and the binding judge missed the audit criterion.

\paragraph{Gates.} Table~\ref{tab:dev-gates} lists every pre-declared DEV gate. The HA precision gate failed (half-width \haHalfwidth{} steps against a limit of 4), so HA is exploratory on the test split by the pre-declared rule. The full-resample control failed (maximum $|C|=\fullMaxC$ against $0.15$). A post-hoc diagnosis separates two components (Figure~\ref{fig:full-control} in the appendix).
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The gate statistic itself.} The maximum comes from spatial relation at $t=6$, a cell informed by five scenes. Simulating exchangeable outcomes with the same design, scenes, eligibility pattern and frozen estimator, the gate statistic has a median of $1.0$ and never fell below $0.15$ in 1{,}000 simulations. As registered, the gate could not have been passed at this sample size, and its failure says nothing about bias.
\item \emph{A presence offset.} Presence, the best-supported cell, shows an offset of about $+0.16$ at every $t$, beyond the simulated null (simulation $p=0.008$). The estimator is unbiased under exchangeability in those simulations and in a unit test, and a FULL branch is a genuinely fresh draw. Its noise seed is hashed per job and never coincides with a base seed, and the marginal presence rates of FULL branches and base seeds are equal ($0.389$ against $0.390$). What differs is agreement: FULL branches agree with one another more often ($0.73$) than the four base seeds do ($0.65$; difference $0.076$, CI $[0.028,0.126]$). FULL images also agree with each base seed, including the two seeds that were never restarted, more often (about $0.70$) than the seeds agree among themselves.
\end{itemize}
Two explanations remain, and we cannot separate them. First, every prompt uses the same four initial-noise seeds, so $A_0$ is measured on one fixed panel of four noise tensors shared by all scenes, and its error is common to all scenes; a seed main effect is not detectable (permutation $p=0.36$). Second, there may be an undetected difference between the base images, generated in an earlier run, and the branch images. A loop-identity check passed on three prompts but not on these images. The offset would make $C(t)$ uniformly too high and absolute $t_{50}$ too early. It affects contrasts less: subtracting it at every guidance scale leaves the HD contrast intact (Section~\ref{sec:resROBUST}). The smallest experiment that would decide between the two explanations is about 400 images: regenerate FULL branches for 50 DEV scenes with the panel seeds 2 and 3 through the branch code (any outcome difference from the stored seed images exposes a pipeline difference), and draw four further base seeds per scene to compare $A_0$ across panels. Both HD presence gates passed (\hdPresTwo{} and \hdPresFifteen{} scenes).

\paragraph{CAG quality guard.} HPS~v2 scored the \hpsPairs{} DEV guard images per $w_{lo}$ against their constant-CFG partners (same prompt and seed). With $w_{lo}=1$ the mean change was \hpsOne{} HPS points (95\% interval \hpsOneLo{} to \hpsOneHi), a drop just beyond the pre-declared $0.5$; with $w_{lo}=3$ it was \hpsThree{} (\hpsThreeLo{} to \hpsThreeHi). By the pre-declared rule $w_{lo}=3$ was adopted before the test split (Figure~\ref{fig:hps}). The switch steps derived from DEV are $s^\star=12$ for prompts whose latest type is presence, binding or spatial relation and $s^\star=21$ for prompts with counts (global $s^\star=\sStarGlobal$).
