\section{Benchmark}\label{sec:benchmark}
Prompts are generated from machine-readable scene graphs with a grammar and a lexicon. Every scene has a canonical prompt (CAN), meaning-preserving variants (synonym paraphrase PAR, mention-order change ORD) and counterfactual variants that change exactly one declared field (colour swap CF-C, relation reversal CF-R, count change CF-N, object deletion CF-O, attribute insertion or removal CF-A). Scenes are split into DEV and TEST at scene level, with object-pair groups confined to one split. The main benchmark has \devScenes{} DEV and \testScenes{} TEST scenes and 1,410 prompts (some variant types do not apply to some scene types). A factorial family of 24 object pairs (8 DEV, 16 TEST) crosses colour, relative size and spatial relation on top of presence, in the style of the task's example ``a small red cube and a large blue sphere'' (192 canonical prompts plus colour-swap, size-swap and relation-reversal counterfactuals).
\paragraph{Variant review and amendment.} All 1,674 prompts and 73 lexicon entries were reviewed against the scene-graph constraints before generation. Two synonym choices were replaced in a dated amendment before any image of the affected prompts was generated: ``golden'' for yellow (the judge's text encoder placed it nearest to brown) and ``on the left/right side of'' (which admits the reference object's own frame); identifiers, constraints and splits were unchanged.
