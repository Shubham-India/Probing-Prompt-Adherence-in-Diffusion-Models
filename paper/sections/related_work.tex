\section{Related work and prior-art status}\label{sec:related}
\paragraph{Diffusion models and guidance.} We use denoising diffusion models \citep{ho2020ddpm}, deterministic DDIM sampling \citep{song2021ddim}, latent diffusion \citep{rombach2022ldm} and classifier-free guidance \citep{ho2022cfg}.
\paragraph{Cross-attention analysis and control.} Cross-attention maps have been used to interpret and edit generations \citep{hertz2023p2p,tang2023daam}, and attention-based objectives or edits have been proposed to improve attribute binding and object presence \citep{chefer2023attend,li2023divide,fu2024gather,rassin2023syngen}.
\paragraph{Timing and re-noising.} Feature emergence along the denoising trajectory has been studied theoretically \citep{li2024critical}; add-noise-then-denoise operations are used for editing and sampling \citep{meng2022sdedit,xu2023restart}; negative prompts have been reported to act with a delay \citep{ban2024negative}. We use re-noising as a \emph{measurement}, not as a sampler.
\paragraph{Predicting outcomes from early signals.} Initial-noise search and early-attention prediction of image quality are close in aim to HB \citep{guo2024initno,diffusionprobe2026}.
\paragraph{Evaluation.} Object-focused and question-answering evaluators and benchmarks \citep{ghosh2023geneval,huang2023t2icompbench,hu2023tifa,kamath2025geneval2} motivate our emphasis on evaluator reliability; our detector-based judge combines open-vocabulary detection \citep{minderer2022owlvit} with CLIP \citep{radford2021clip}.

\paragraph{Stages, guidance schedules and timing (Revision 3 prior art).} Switching prompts over denoising intervals localises layout, shape and detail to successive stages \citep{patashnik2023mixing}; stage-specific embeddings control different attributes \citep{zhang2023prospect}; text conditioning has been reported to matter most early \citep{balaji2022ediffi}. Guidance applied in a limited interval improves sample quality \citep{kynkaanniemi2024interval}, annealing the condition early restores diversity \citep{sadat2024cads}, and per-step guidance chosen with latent evaluators is prompt-dependent \citep{dynamiccfg2025}; the latter is the closest competitor of CAG. A single attention-based refinement step works best at a constraint-dependent time \citep{grimal2025step}, and attention can read and steer object size and position \citep{epstein2023selfguidance}. Our questions are narrower: a causal, chance-corrected persistence step per constraint type, its dependence on CFG, its attention signature, and a schedule read off the measurement rather than tuned on adherence. Seed diversity uses DINOv2 embeddings \citep{oquab2024dinov2}.

\paragraph{Verification status.} Full texts of the diffusion, attention-control, binding and benchmark papers above were read from the project's PDF collection. The timing, noise-search, early-prediction and negative-prompt works were checked at abstract or search-result level only, and several metadata fields are marked in \texttt{references.bib} for re-checking. The gap addressed by HA, constraint-type-specific interventional persistence curves on controlled scene graphs, is a candidate gap \textbf{found in a narrow search, not an established one}.
